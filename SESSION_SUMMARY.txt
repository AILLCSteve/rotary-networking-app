PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================
AI-powered business networking platform for Rotary Club events that matches
members using OpenAI embeddings + GPT-4 to create personalized connection
recommendations.

TECH STACK:
- Backend: Node.js + Express
- Database: PostgreSQL (Neon cloud)
- AI: OpenAI (text-embedding-3-small + GPT-4o/GPT-3.5-turbo)
- Deployment: Render Web Service (paid tier)
- GitHub: https://github.com/AILLCSteve/rotary-networking-app

✅ 3. CONFIDENCE SCORING SYSTEM
   Quantifies reliability of AI insights with 0-100% transparency score

   Implementation (server.js:1794-1843):
   ────────────────────────────────────
   calculateConfidenceScore() analyzes:

   - Data Quality (40% weight):
     * Creative ideas: 5 pts each (max 20)
     * Industry research depth: 10 pts if >500 chars
     * Company research depth: 10 pts if >500 chars

   - Fact-Check Score (30% weight):
     * Starts at 100
     * -10 pts per removed claim
     * -3 pts per inference-only claim

   - Research Depth (30% weight):
     * 15 pts: Industry research completed
     * 15 pts: Company research completed
     * 10 pts: Web research findings available

   Rating Thresholds:
   - High: 80%+ (comprehensive research + verified facts)
   - Medium: 60-79% (good research depth)
   - Moderate: <60% (recommendations based on available data)

   Metadata Stored (server.js:1085-1094):
   ────────────────────────────────────
   scoreData now includes:
   - confidence_score: { overall, data_quality, fact_check, research_depth, rating, explanation }
   - ai_persona: "Strategic Growth Advisor"
   - research_timestamp: ISO timestamp

   Impact: Users see exactly how reliable each match recommendation is

✅ RESEARCH-BACKED SELECTION FLOW (NEW APPROACH)
   Changed from "score first, research later" to "research determines winners"

   OLD FLOW (Research didn't inform selection):
   1. Score all candidates
   2. Pick top 3 by score
   3. Research those 3
   4. Generate intros

   NEW FLOW (Research drives selection):
   1. Quick-score all candidates (no research yet)
   2. Select top 10 candidates for research
   3. Research ALL 10 for creative collaboration opportunities
   4. RE-SCORE with research included
   5. Pick final top 3 based on research-backed scores
   6. Generate intros (research already done)
